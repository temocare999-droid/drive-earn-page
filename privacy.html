Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This policy explains what information this website collects, why, and what you can do about it. We have tried to keep it in plain language rather than legal boilerplate.

Who we are

This is an independent website that publishes information about becoming a rideshare or delivery driver in the United States. We are not a rideshare or delivery company, we do not employ or contract drivers, and we are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any such company. When you apply through a referral link on this site, we may receive a referral bonus from the platform at no cost to you.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not operate a contact form or newsletter. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, mailing addresses, payment details, government identifiers, or any documents relating to your driver application.

Like most websites, our host records standard technical information automatically when a page is requested. This can include your IP address, browser type and version, device type, the page you requested, and the page you arrived from. This information is used to keep the site working and to understand roughly how many people visit.

The earnings estimator

The estimator on our home page runs entirely inside your browser. The values you enter — hours, market type, fuel economy, and gas price — are never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and are not stored after you close the page.

Advertising and analytics

We advertise this site through Google Ads. If you arrived here from an advertisement, Google may set cookies or use similar technologies to measure whether the advertisement led to a useful visit. We may also use an analytics tool to see aggregate traffic patterns, such as which pages are read most.

These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies, which we do not control. You can review how Google handles data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can manage your own ad personalization settings at myadcenter.google.com.

When you leave this site

Clicking a referral link takes you to a third party's website. From that point, that company's privacy policy governs what is collected and how it is used — including everything you submit as part of a driver application. We never see your application, its contents, or its outcome. If you have questions about how the platform handles your data, contact that company directly.

Cookies

We do not set cookies ourselves for tracking purposes. Cookies that may be present come from the advertising and analytics services described above. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies in its settings; doing so will not prevent you from reading anything on this site.

Children

This site is intended for adults considering independent driving work. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Your choices

Because we do not collect personal information directly, there is generally nothing for us to retrieve, correct, or erase. Depending on where you live — for example under the California Consumer Privacy Act or the GDPR — you may have rights regarding data held by the advertising and analytics providers named above. Those requests are best directed to those providers, since they hold the data. If you believe we hold information about you and want it removed, contact us and we will look into it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with an updated date at the top. Material changes will be reflected in the summary above rather than buried in the text.

Contact

For any question about this policy, reach us through the contact details published on our home page. We are a small independent operation, so please allow a few days for a reply.
